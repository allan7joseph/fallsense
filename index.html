1 · Blank test → noise floor and LOD

Put the phone face-up on a firm table and do not touch it for 30 s. This is the "blank" of Class 15: LOD = meanblank + 3.3·SDblank of the post-impact motion metric. A phone lying on the floor reads below this LOD; a person lying on the floor (breathing, small movements) reads above it.

2 · Six-position test → offset (bias) and sensitivity (gain)

Hold the phone still with each axis pointing up, then down (6 × 5 s). Accuracy is checked against ±1 g.

Labelled recording (data collection protocol)

Safety: simulated falls only onto a ≥10 cm crash mat, supervised, by the team members only. Phone drops only onto a mat or carpet, or with a case on.
